How VeriFact AI Works

  1. TF-IDF Feature Extraction: News text is normalized, cleaned, and converted into n-gram word vector representations.
  2. Passive-Aggressive Machine Learning: Uses continuous online learning to classify high-dimensional text data into Authentic vs Fake.
  3. Sensationalism & Tone Engine: Analyzes capitalization ratio, dramatic punctuation, hyperbole, and medical/financial scam triggers.
  4. Live Google RSS Real News Matcher: Extracts core entity keywords and pulls verified real news links for immediate user reference.
